Bot chess

Play a standard game against a bot on this site.

Legal moves are enforced, including check, castling, en passant, and promotion. The bot is Stockfish 19 Lite in a worker. Its strength control is UCI_Elo from 1320 to 3190. Those values are the engine's limits, not a measured rating. This build does not accept a target below 1320. Press Start game before any move. When the game ends you can download the PGN or discard the result. Save to account keeps the full game after you sign in and click Join Chess. Automatic saving stays off unless you turn it on for that account.

Account settings

Automatically save bot-game results is off by default. It is stored with the Chess account, not on this device. Sign in and click Join Chess before turning it on.
