Standard piece drawings
=======================

The Standard set is the Cburnett chess piece set from Wikimedia Commons,
drawn by Colin M.L. Burnett (Wikimedia user Cburnett).

Source files:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:SVG_chess_pieces
https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/42/Chess_klt45.svg
https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/15/Chess_qlt45.svg
https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/72/Chess_rlt45.svg
https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b1/Chess_blt45.svg
https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/ef/Chess_nlt45.svg

The paths are recolored in the app for the ink, ivory, and sky piece colors.
The shapes are otherwise the Cburnett drawings.

Bold and Contour
================

Bold and Contour are original silhouettes drawn for this project. They are
not the Cburnett set. Contour is an outline treatment of the Bold silhouettes.
