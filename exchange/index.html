Exchange

This page is reserved for a simulated ratings trading exchange connected to the marketplace and the community.

It is planned and is not operational. There is no market engine. This page shows no orders, prices, volume, accounts, or balances, and it has not issued a balance.

The intended simulated currency is AMU. The intended allotment is one trillion (1,000,000,000,000) AMU per account. Accounts are not meant to issue themselves an unlimited amount. AMU has no real-money value. The exchange is not for deposits, cashouts, or real trading.

Separate markets are intended for official rating formats, Chess.com ratings, Lichess ratings, and future ratings from this site. An actual rating is distinct from a simulated price. This site does not claim ownership of any player and does not claim an endorsement from any player, Chess.com, or Lichess.
